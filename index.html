About the study

Hestia is a research project at The University of Texas at Dallas studying whether everyday household signals — ambient sounds (water running, kitchen activity, footstep[...]

  • Older adults living independently — recognizing daily routines and flagging deviations from the usual pattern, such as missed meals, disrupted sleep, or unusual late-[...]
  • Remote workers living alone — structuring the day, identifying skipped meals or irregular sleep.
  • Self-reflection — a private summary of how the day is actually spent.

All processing runs locally. No raw video is stored, no cloud uploads, no speech transcription.

What you'd be doing

  • We place 3 small microphone sensors in your home — one each in the bedroom, bathroom, and kitchen.
  • We place one camera and one WiFi sensor in the living room only — the camera outputs only a simple activity label, never video or images.
  • You carry a small study phone (Pixel 8) during the day for its motion sensors.
  • You live normally for 4 to 12 days (you pick the length).
  • You keep a simple activity diary (~5 minutes per day) noting when you slept, ate, showered, etc.
  • At the end, we remove the sensors and delete anything you don't want us to keep.

Privacy and data handling

Here is exactly what each sensor collects:

  • Audio — the microphones record ambient sound. For analysis, the researcher (Xiao Yan) may listen to short segments to verify sound types. Speech content is not tran[...]
  • Camera — placed in the living room only (never in bedrooms or bathrooms). Video is processed on-device into a simple activity label (sitting, standing, walking, lyi[...]
  • WiFi sensor — only wireless signal/motion patterns are recorded. It does not capture your internet traffic or any content.
  • Study phone — only motion sensors (accelerometer / gyroscope) are recorded. No calls, messages, apps, or location are collected.

None of this data will be published, shared with third parties, sold, or uploaded to any public dataset.

You may request deletion of your data at any time.

Who we're looking for

  • Preferably living alone (single-resident homes simplify the data analysis).
  • Your home has a bedroom, bathroom, and kitchen where we can place one small sensor each.
  • You're comfortable keeping a brief daily diary.
  • You're located in the Dallas / UT Dallas area (or somewhere I can visit to set up).
