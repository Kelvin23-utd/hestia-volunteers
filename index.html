About the study

Hestia is a research project at The University of Texas at Dallas studying whether ambient household sounds (water running, kitchen activity, footsteps, doors, etc.) can b[...]

  • Elderly living independently — detecting missed meals, falls, or prolonged inactivity without cameras.
  • Remote workers living alone — structuring the day, identifying skipped meals or irregular sleep.
  • Self-reflection — a private summary of how the day is actually spent.

All processing runs locally. No cameras, no cloud uploads, no speech transcription.

What you'd be doing

  • We place 3 small microphone sensors in your home — one each in the bedroom, bathroom, and kitchen.
  • You live normally for 3 to 5 days (you pick the length).
  • You keep a simple activity diary (~5 minutes per day) noting when you slept, ate, showered, etc.
  • At the end, we remove the sensors and delete anything you don't want us to keep.

Privacy and data handling

The sensors record audio. For analysis, the researcher (Xiao Yan) may listen to short segments to verify sound types.

The recordings will not be published, shared with third parties, sold, or uploaded to any public dataset. Speech content will not be transcribed or extracted.You may request deletion of your data at any time.

Who we're looking for

  • Preferably living alone (single-resident homes simplify the data analysis).
  • Your home has a bedroom, bathroom, and kitchen where we can place one small sensor each.
  • You're comfortable keeping a brief daily diary.
  • You're located in the Dallas / UT Dallas area (or somewhere I can visit to set up).

关于本研究

Hestia 是 德州大学达拉斯分校 (UT Dallas) 的一项研究项目，研究能否通过家中的环境声音（水流声、厨房活动、脚步声、开关门声等）��[...]

  • 独居老人——在不使用摄像头的前提下，识别漏餐、跌倒或长时间异常静止。
  • 独自在家的远程工作者——辅助安排作息，识别漏餐或睡眠紊乱。
  • 个人作息回顾——以保护隐私的方式了解自己一天的实际情况。

所有处理均在本地完成。不使用摄像头，不上传云端，不转录语音。

你需要做什么

  • 我们会在你家中放置 3 个小型麦克风传感器——卧室、卫生间、厨房各一个。
  • 你像平常一样生活 3 到 5 天（时长由你决定）。
  • 你每天花约 5 分钟简单记录活动日志（例如何时睡觉、吃饭、洗澡等）。
  • 实验结束后，我们会取走设备，并删除你不希望保留的任何数据。

隐私与数据处理

传感器会录制音频。为完成分析，研究者（Xiao Yan）可能会听取部分片段以确认声音类型。

录音不会被公开发表、分享给第三方、出售，也不会上传至任何公开数据集。不会对语音内容进行转录或提取。

你可以随时要求删除全部数据。

适合哪些志愿者

  • 最好是独居（单人住所更便于数据分析）。
  • 家中有卧室、卫生间和厨房各一个，可以各放一个小传感器。
  • 愿意每天花几分钟记录简短的活动日志。
  • 位于达拉斯 / UT Dallas 周边地区（或我能上门安装的位置）。
